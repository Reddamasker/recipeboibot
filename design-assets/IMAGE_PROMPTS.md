## recipe-hero-3d-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide hero artwork for a mobile personal recipe collection app, not a full UI mockup. Create a highly art-directed premium 3D food still life, landscape 3:2 composition. A sculptural pale-lavender ceramic plate tilted gently toward camera holds jewel-like roasted beet wedges, soft ivory cheese, walnut pieces and fresh arugula. Two small blank translucent lilac glass recipe cards float behind the plate, suggesting saved recipes, with only subtle embossed abstract lines, NO letters. A slender curled lime-green ingredient ribbon arcs around one edge of the composition. Strong central silhouette readable in a small mobile crop, generous breathing space around the subject. Colour world: deep aubergine #302537 studio background, lilac ceramic and glass, beet berry #A4415C, tiny electric lime #DDF18A accents. Beautiful believable food texture, matte glazed ceramic versus softly refractive frosted glass, physically based 3D materials, subtle caustic light on background, tactile contact shadows, dramatic broad softbox side-light and delicate rim light. Sophisticated editorial food art, playful through composition not faces, high-end render, no plastic toy food, no generic gradient blobs. Keep all main objects fully inside frame. No text, logos, brand name, watermarks, UI buttons, random strings, people, hands, or extra props.

## beet-salad-editorial-v1.png

Use case: product-mockup. Input image is a STYLE AND FOOD REFERENCE, not an edit target. Generate a companion editorial food image for a recipe detail page. Same tactile pale-lavender ceramic plate and delicious roasted beet wedges, fresh arugula, soft ivory cheese and walnut pieces as the reference. Entire round plate fully visible, centered overhead flat-lay, close but with comfortable margins. Quiet pale-lilac studio backdrop #E7DEF0, subtle physically realistic contact shadow, soft daylight-like studio light from upper left, rich natural ruby beet colour and crisp herb details. Photorealistic appetizing ingredient textures with the beautiful controlled lighting of a premium 3D food campaign. Wide landscape 3:2 composition suited to a mobile recipe header. Only plate and food. No glass cards, no floating ribbons, no typography, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, cutlery, hands or unrelated props.

The hero image was supplied as the style and food reference for the companion image. Material effects, depth, refraction and caustics are baked into these raster images; they are not interactive shaders or runtime 3D scenes.
